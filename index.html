About the Universe

Where Play
Becomes the Art

Art has the power to reconnect us with curiosity, imagination, and freedom. Universo Yica is an evolving artistic world where painting, sculpture, immersive installations, and playful experiences come together to create moments of wonder.

Inspired by childhood creativity and the courage to see the world differently, each work invites visitors to pause, explore, and become active participants rather than passive observers.

Recurring characters, symbolic eyes, vibrant colors, and reimagined cultural references form a visual language that crosses generations and cultures. Every piece is part of a larger story celebrating joy, emotion, and the limitless potential of human creativity.

Rather than offering answers, Yica creates spaces where questions, play, and imagination become the artwork itself.

Mission

To inspire people of all ages to rediscover their creativity and remember that imagination is one of our greatest human strengths.

Vision

To build an artistic universe that transforms galleries, public spaces, and cultural institutions into places of participation, connection, and shared creativity.

Philosophy

Emotion over perfection. Every work begins with feeling, not formula. The imperfect line, the unexpected color, the playful accident — these are where truth lives.
